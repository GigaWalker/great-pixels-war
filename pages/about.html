THE

GREAT PIXELS WAR

An open-world turn-based strategy game built on real satellite maps. Fight for territory, form alliances, bid on cities, and leave your mark on the real world — one pixel at a time.

HOW TO PLAY
⚡
QUICK MATCH
1Click Play → Quick Match
2Server finds the best available game for your rank
3Deploy your forces on the starting city
4Take turns: move units, capture cities, attack enemies
5Last player standing wins — earn Bronze and XP
🌍
SEASON WAR
1At season start: bid Gold to claim a starting city
2Each turn: spend income on units, move, attack
3Form alliances — diplomacy is as powerful as armies
4Go offline? Bot defends your territory automatically
5Season ends — top players earn Diamonds and medals
📖
STORY MODE
1Choose a chapter from the current season's campaign
2Select solo, co-op (2–4), or competitive (up to 8)
3Follow the narrative objectives — not just survival
4Complete all 3 chapters to unlock the season finale
5Earn Silver, Bronze, story medals and lore entries
CURRENCY GUIDE
🥉
BRONZE
Earned every game — participation rewards, season minimum. Buys Common tier cosmetics. City bidding: no.
🥈
SILVER
Earned from wins, top-50 season finishes, story mode. Buys Veteran tier cosmetics. City bidding: no.
🥇
GOLD
Earned from ranked play, top-10 season finishes. Buys Elite tier cosmetics. Also used for Season War city bidding.
💎
DIAMONDS
Bought with real money or earned as top season rewards (1 minimum per participant). Buys Command tier cosmetics, Gold VIP, bundles, and exclusive items. Diamond VIP is real money only.
